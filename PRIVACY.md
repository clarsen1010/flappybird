# Blappy Fird: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Blappy Fird has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device is what the optional Friends leaderboard needs.

- **Friends leaderboard (optional).** If you pick a player name, the game stores one small record in this app's database on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): the name you chose, your best score in each game mode, your best score today, this week and this month with their dates, when you last played, and the list of players you added as friends. Other players of the game can see that record, and so can the developer. Apple ties the record to your iCloud account with an identifier made for this app only; the game never sees your Apple ID, your email address or your real name. If you never pick a name, nothing is stored there.
- **Deleting it.** In the game, open Settings, tap NAME and choose "Delete my name and scores". The record is removed from the server right away.
- **Scores and settings** are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your scores, best runs and friends list (and which friends you removed) also sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). The developer cannot see that data.
- **Play log.** Test versions of the game (TestFlight) have a Logs switch in Settings. When it is on, the game writes a play log to the app's own folder on your device (Files > On My iPhone > Blappy Fird). It stays on your device unless you choose to share it. The App Store version has the log off and does not show the switch.
- **Diagnostics.** Apple may give the app performance and crash reports (MetricKit). The app saves these in its own folder on your device only.
- **Nothing is sold or shared.** The leaderboard data is used only to show the leaderboard. Apple hosts the database; no other company receives anything.

**Sharing your name.** SHARE NAME in the Friends panel opens your iPhone's own share sheet with your player name in a short message; it goes only where you choose to send it.

**Player names.** A player name is the only thing anyone types into the game. Please do not use your real name.

**Children.** The game asks for no personal details from anyone. The leaderboard stores only what is listed above, for any player who picks a name.

**Questions, or a name you want removed.** Open an issue at https://github.com/clarsen1010/flappybird/issues

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
