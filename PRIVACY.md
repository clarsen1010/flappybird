# Blappy Fird: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

Blappy Fird does not collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** The app has no sign-in, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking code.
- **Scores and settings** are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your best score and best runs sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). The developer cannot see that data.
- **Play log (Settings > Logs).** When it is on, the game writes a play log to the app's own folder on your device (Files > On My iPhone > Blappy Fird). It stays on your device unless you choose to share it. It is off by default in the App Store version.
- **Diagnostics.** Apple may give the app performance and crash reports (MetricKit). The app saves these in its own folder on your device only.

**Children.** The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Questions.** Open an issue at https://github.com/clarsen1010/flappybird/issues

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
